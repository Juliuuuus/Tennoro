Why Tennoro?

A statistical, transparent and reproducible approach.

Most tennis predictions rely on a quick look at the odds or a manual review of a few high-profile matches.

Tennoro takes a different approach. Every day, the model automatically analyses ATP and WTA matches using thousands of historical matches, players’ recent form, surface-specific performance, head-to-head records and Elo ratings.

The aim is not to publish as many picks as possible, but to identify matches where several indicators point in the same direction.

Published picks are then tracked automatically to measure accuracy, ROI and performance over time.

This methodology keeps the approach consistent, transparent and reproducible.
